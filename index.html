About the Project

This webpage presents the assignments completed as part of the Digital Humanities coursework. The project explores the application of Natural Language Processing, Artificial Intelligence, and Digital Humanities methods to English language and literary texts.

1. NLP – English Stop Words

This project contains a sample collection of English stop words for Natural Language Processing. These words can be used during text preprocessing and linguistic analysis.
